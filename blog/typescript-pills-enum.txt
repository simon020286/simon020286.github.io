d:T304e,<p>TypeScript offre il costrutto <code>enum</code> per definire insiemi di valori nominativi, simili alle costanti. Tuttavia, il loro utilizzo presenta diversi svantaggi che spesso li rendono una scelta subottimale rispetto ad alternative più moderne e flessibili. In questo articolo, esploreremo i principali problemi degli <code>enum</code> e le soluzioni alternative.</p>
<h2>Perché non usarli</h2>
<p>Gli enum in TypeScript non vengono semplicemente rimossi in fase di compilazione, ma generano codice JavaScript. Ad esempio:</p>
<pre class="shiki shiki-themes material-theme-darker material-theme-lighter" style="background-color:#212121;--shiki-dark-bg:#FAFAFA;color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE" tabindex="0"><code><span class="line"><span style="color:#C792EA;--shiki-dark:#9C3EDA">enum</span><span style="color:#FFCB6B;--shiki-dark:#E2931D"> Status</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> {</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">  Pending</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">,</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">  Approved</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">,</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">  Rejected</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">,</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">}</span></span></code></pre>
<p>Viene compilato così:</p>
<pre class="shiki shiki-themes material-theme-darker material-theme-lighter" style="background-color:#212121;--shiki-dark-bg:#FAFAFA;color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE" tabindex="0"><code><span class="line"><span style="color:#C792EA;--shiki-dark:#9C3EDA">var</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE"> Status</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">;</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">(</span><span style="color:#C792EA;--shiki-dark:#9C3EDA">function</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> (</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;font-style:italic;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE;--shiki-dark-font-style:italic">Status</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">)</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> {</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">  Status</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">[(</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">Status</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">[</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">Pending</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">] </span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">=</span><span style="color:#F78C6C;--shiki-dark:#F76D47"> 0</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">)] </span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">=</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> "</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">Pending</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">;</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">  Status</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">[(</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">Status</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">[</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">Approved</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">] </span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">=</span><span style="color:#F78C6C;--shiki-dark:#F76D47"> 1</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">)] </span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">=</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> "</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">Approved</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">;</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">  Status</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">[(</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">Status</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">[</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">Rejected</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">] </span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">=</span><span style="color:#F78C6C;--shiki-dark:#F76D47"> 2</span><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">)] </span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">=</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> "</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">Rejected</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">;</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">}</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">)(Status </span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">||</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE"> (Status </span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">=</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> {}</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">))</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">;</span></span></code></pre>
<p>Questo significa che gli <code>enum</code> generano codice runtime aggiuntivo, aumentando il peso del bundle e introducendo potenziali problemi di performance rispetto a soluzioni più leggere come le <code>union types</code>.<br>
A differenza di altre strutture dati di TypeScript, gli enum non si comportano come oggetti JavaScript standard. Questo può portare a problemi di compatibilità quando si lavora con librerie JavaScript esistenti o quando si effettua la serializzazione/deserializzazione di dati JSON.<br>
Ad esempio:</p>
<pre class="shiki shiki-themes material-theme-darker material-theme-lighter" style="background-color:#212121;--shiki-dark-bg:#FAFAFA;color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE" tabindex="0"><code><span class="line"><span style="color:#C792EA;--shiki-dark:#9C3EDA">const</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE"> status</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">:</span><span style="color:#FFCB6B;--shiki-dark:#E2931D"> Status</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> =</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE"> JSON</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">.</span><span style="color:#82AAFF;--shiki-dark:#6182B8">parse</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">(</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">'</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">{"status": "APPROVED"}</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">'</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE">)</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">;</span></span></code></pre>
<p>Questo codice causerà un errore, perché TypeScript non riconosce la stringa come un valore valido dell'<code>enum</code>.<br>
In ultimo poiché gli enum vengono convertiti in oggetti JavaScript, i nomi delle chiavi non possono essere abbreviati dai minificatori, aumentando inutilmente la dimensione del codice finale.</p>
<h2>Alternative agli Enum</h2>
<h3>1. Union Types</h3>
<p>Le union types sono un'alternativa più leggera ed efficace:</p>
<pre class="shiki shiki-themes material-theme-darker material-theme-lighter" style="background-color:#212121;--shiki-dark-bg:#FAFAFA;color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE" tabindex="0"><code><span class="line"><span style="color:#C792EA;--shiki-dark:#9C3EDA">type</span><span style="color:#FFCB6B;--shiki-dark:#E2931D"> Status</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> =</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> "</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">PENDING</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> |</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> "</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">APPROVED</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> |</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> "</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">REJECTED</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">;</span></span></code></pre>
<p>Questo approccio offre:</p>
<ul>
<li>Zero codice runtime (TypeScript lo rimuove completamente in fase di compilazione).</li>
<li>Maggiore compatibilità con JSON e librerie JavaScript.</li>
<li>Controllo dei tipi e autocompletamento senza generare overhead.</li>
</ul>
<h3>2. Oggetti Come Enum</h3>
<p>Se si desidera mantenere la struttura chiave-valore, è possibile usare oggetti:</p>
<pre class="shiki shiki-themes material-theme-darker material-theme-lighter" style="background-color:#212121;--shiki-dark-bg:#FAFAFA;color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE" tabindex="0"><code><span class="line"><span style="color:#C792EA;--shiki-dark:#9C3EDA">const</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE"> Status </span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">=</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> {</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">  Pending</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">:</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> "</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">PENDING</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">,</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">  Approved</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">:</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> "</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">APPROVED</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">,</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#F07178;--shiki-dark:#E53935">  Rejected</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">:</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> "</span><span style="color:#C3E88D;--shiki-dark:#91B859">REJECTED</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">"</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">,</span></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">}</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;font-style:italic;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5;--shiki-dark-font-style:italic"> as</span><span style="color:#C792EA;--shiki-dark:#9C3EDA"> const</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">;</span></span>
<span class="line"></span>
<span class="line"><span style="color:#C792EA;--shiki-dark:#9C3EDA">type</span><span style="color:#FFCB6B;--shiki-dark:#E2931D"> Status</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> =</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE"> (</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">typeof</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE"> Status)[</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">keyof</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5"> typeof</span><span style="color:#EEFFFF;--shiki-dark:#90A4AE"> Status]</span><span style="color:#89DDFF;--shiki-dark:#39ADB5">;</span></span></code></pre>
<p>Questo metodo mantiene tutti i vantaggi delle union types e offre una rappresentazione più naturale in JavaScript.</p>
<p>Sebbene gli enum possano sembrare utili, i loro svantaggi in termini di output JavaScript, compatibilità e minificazione ne rendono sconsigliato l'uso nella maggior parte dei casi.
